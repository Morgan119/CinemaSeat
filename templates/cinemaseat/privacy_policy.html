Privacy Policy

CinemaSeat respects your privacy and only uses the information needed to provide a smooth booking experience.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, booking details, payment method and selected seat information when you create an account or complete a reservation.

How we use it

Your data is used to create and manage bookings, confirm tickets, handle support requests, improve service quality, and communicate important updates about your reservation.

Payment data

We do not store full card details. Mobile money and card references are used only for payment verification and ticketing purposes.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect your account and ticket information from unauthorised access.

Data retention

Booking and account information is retained for as long as needed to provide service, resolve disputes, and meet legal or operational obligations.
